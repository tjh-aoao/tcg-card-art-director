# 《域·维度战争》卡图视觉圣经

Use this as the default style control layer. Revise it when the project gains a formal brand bible.

## Project Identity

Zone War is a two-player TCG built around domain sovereignty, position combat, energy rhythm, and resonance formations.

Core gameplay pillars:
- 域能节奏: energy, cost, maintenance, acceleration, exhaustion.
- 站位攻防: frontline/backline, protection, breakthrough, movement, target switching.
- 共鸣阵收益: spatial patterns, resonance points, formation completion, risk/reward.
- 权值 ZP: sovereignty, presence, territory pressure, accumulated advantage.

Art must make these pillars visible over time.

## Visual Thesis

Zone War cards should feel like conflicts over living domains: characters and forces are not floating in generic space; they shape, claim, distort, defend, or break territory.

Default visual keywords:
- domain, boundary, resonance, geometry, pressure, ritual, terrain logic, tactical pose, energy economy.

Avoid making the whole game read as one-note "dark magic" or "generic anime fantasy." Use varied attributes and faction languages while preserving domain/resonance logic.

## Card-Entry Presentation Rule

Default card art should read like a playable card appearing or activating, not a quiet story illustration.

Use:
- Higher saturation and stronger value contrast than ordinary concept art.
- Large readable subject blocks: hair mass, crown, cloak, wings, shield, weapon, tail, leaf cluster, core, or halo.
- Strong outer contour lines and rim light around the head, shoulders, weapon, and main prop.
- Bright eye highlights, chest/core highlights, crystal highlights, or domain-energy highlights.
- Simplified stage-like backgrounds: colored energy field, domain platform, summoning circle, clean battlefield lane, or broad gradient.
- A pose captured at an entrance, cast, attack, block, landing, command, transformation, or effect activation moment.

Avoid:
- Low-saturation gray, dusty pink, beige, old-paper, or evenly muted palettes as the default look.
- Backgrounds evenly filled with small branches, leaves, rubble, wires, smoke, spores, particles, or texture.
- Soft silhouettes that merge with the environment.
- Static portrait poses when the card needs a readable effect or unit identity.

For green/forest themes, the default background can be a green energy stage, simple root platform, or clean shelterbelt silhouette. Dense branch detail should be reserved for cards whose identity is specifically "forest maze", "ambush", or "terrain".

## Card Type Visual Rules

### 域主

Purpose: the player's chosen identity and strategic thesis.

Must show:
- Strong silhouette and face/identity readability.
- A domain-signature motif behind or around the figure.
- One clear "authority" gesture, stance, weapon, or environmental response.
- If awakening is relevant, include a latent second-state cue rather than random glow.

Avoid:
- Passive portrait with no domain control.
- Background that does not reveal faction or strategy.

### 普通域灵

Purpose: tactical unit that participates in combat and ZP accumulation.

Must show:
- Action-ready pose or role-specific body language.
- Attribute/faction material cue.
- Scale readable enough to distinguish unit class.

Avoid:
- Too much cinematic blur.
- Abstract entity that cannot be recognized at table distance.

### 共鸣域灵

Purpose: unit whose value depends on spatial formation.

Must show:
- A visible formation clue: orbit points, floor marks, linked nodes, constellation geometry, mirrored allies, or spatial anchor.
- The subject as 阵眼 or a clear participant in an array.
- Energy flow that implies conditional completion, not free power.

Avoid:
- Random mandala that hides body shape.
- Coordinates or nodes so tiny they fail on print.

### 通常域痕

Purpose: instant event, skill, omen, or tactical trace.

Must show:
- A decisive moment with clear before/after implication.
- Cause and effect in one image.

Avoid:
- A static object with no event.
- Persistent terrain if the card is not a 结界域痕.

### 结界域痕

Purpose: persistent field, law, boundary, terrain, or ongoing condition.

Must show:
- A stable zone effect.
- How the field changes movement, protection, pressure, or resource flow.

Avoid:
- Pure explosion or one-time spell impact.

## Series Bible Fields

For every new series/theme, define:

- Series name.
- One-sentence fantasy.
- Gameplay identity.
- Emotional promise.
- 3 visual pillars.
- Palette: primary, secondary, accent, prohibited.
- Materials.
- Silhouette language.
- Large silhouette blocks: hair/crown/cloak/weapon/core/other.
- Motifs.
- Lighting rule.
- Background/world rule.
- Energy-stage simplification rule.
- Rarity escalation.
- Card-type variants.
- Do list.
- Don't list.
- 3 sample cards.

## Readability Checklist

- Main subject readable at thumbnail size.
- Subject has large silhouette blocks, not only small decorative details.
- Saturation and value contrast are strong enough for a card pack/product look.
- Strong outer contour, rim light, eye highlight, or core highlight separates the subject from the background.
- Background is simpler than the subject and does not compete with the silhouette.
- Pose reads as card entrance, activation, attack, defense, or transformation when appropriate.
- Card type identifiable without reading text.
- Attribute/faction cue visible.
- Important face/symbol/action not covered by name box, rules box, stats, or resonance marks.
- No tiny required text inside the illustration.
- Grayscale value separation still works.
- The art does not imply a rule the card lacks.
